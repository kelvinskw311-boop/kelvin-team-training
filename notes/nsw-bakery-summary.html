Bread and Bakery Sector Action Plan: our summary

面包与烘焙行业行动计划：我们的摘要（中英对照）

This is our own short summary of two sections (Retail, Foodservice), written in our words for training. It is not a translation of the report. Please read the original for full detail: NSW Love Food Hate Waste, Bread and Bakery Sector Action Plan. The report is by Rawtec and Stop Food Waste Australia and is Australian, so use it as general good practice only.这是我们为培训用自己的话写的两个章节（零售、餐饮服务）简短摘要，不是报告的翻译。完整内容请阅读原文（上面的链接）。该报告由 Rawtec 与 Stop Food Waste Australia 编写，针对澳大利亚，只作通用的良好做法参考。

Retail 零售

Why bread is wasted 为什么会浪费

  • Unsold bread is the biggest waste source in retail. The report puts retail at roughly 104,000 tonnes a year across bakery waste and animal-feed diversion.零售端没卖掉的面包是最大的浪费来源，报告估计每年约 10.4 万吨（含当作废料或动物饲料的部分）。
  • In most places suppliers take back unsold bread. Return rates are roughly 12 to 20% of items where this is allowed, against about 2 to 6% in South Australia, which restricts it. That suggests the return rule shapes how much gets baked.多数地区供应商会收回没卖掉的面包，退货率约 12 至 20%；南澳限制这种做法，退货率只有约 2 至 6%。可见退货规定会影响烘焙数量。
  • Bread carries a high mark-up, so one extra sale can look worth throwing away several loaves. Shoppers also expect full shelves until closing, which pushes stores to over-bake.面包毛利高，多卖一个可能抵得上丢掉好几个。顾客又期待打烊前货架仍是满的，促使门店多烤。

What the report suggests 报告的建议

  • Review sale-and-return rules, and where there are no rules, let producers and retailers share ownership of waste so both gain from cutting it.检讨退货规定；没有规定的地方，让生产商与零售商共同承担浪费，一起受益于减废。
  • Use point-of-sale data to find the balance between a full shelf and low waste (a pilot with a retailer).用销售点数据找出“货架饱满”与“低损耗”之间的平衡（与一家零售商试点）。
  • Give retailers a best-practice guide that shows the true cost of waste, with a calculator. An example given is collapsible shelving at a bakery chain, estimated to save about 5 tonnes of bread per unit each year.为零售商提供最佳实践指南，展示浪费的真实成本并附计算器。例子：某烘焙连锁店使用可折叠货架，估计每个货架每年可省约 5 吨面包。

Foodservice 餐饮服务

Why bread is wasted 为什么会浪费

  • Plate waste is the main source. Foodservice is estimated at around 54,000 tonnes a year, and much of it is bread left uneaten.客人吃剩是主要来源，餐饮服务估计每年约 5.4 万吨，其中很多是没吃的面包。
  • Bread is often served as a filler or basket to give customers a sense of value, then left. Portions can also be too large, and institutions may provide more carbohydrate than people eat.面包常被当作配菜或面包篮来显示物超所值，结果吃不完。份量可能过大，机构供应的碳水也可能多于实际所需。

What the report suggests 报告的建议

  • Run waste audits, work out the true cost of waste, review menus and portions, ask customers how much bread they want, reuse leftover bread, and offer takeaway containers.做损耗盘点、算出浪费的真实成本、检视菜单和份量、问客人想要多少面包、再利用剩余面包、提供外带容器。
  • Institutions should compare the carbohydrate they plan per day with the bread actually offered. One hotel example: cutting the carbohydrate portion from 80 g to 60 g per person halved its food waste and saved almost $860,000 in 2018 to 19.机构应对照每日计划的碳水量与实际供应的面包。酒店例子：把每人碳水份量从 80 克减到 60 克，浪费减半，2018–19 年省下近 86 万澳元。

For our stores 对我们门店的意思

  • A full display and low waste pull in opposite directions. Use sell-through, waste % and sold-out times by day type (Unit 1) to find the balance rather than guessing.饱满陈列与低损耗是互相拉扉的。用第 1 单元的售罄率、损耗率和各日型的售罄时间去找平衡，不要凭感觉。
  • Record every discard (Unit 2), because the true cost of waste only shows up when the numbers are real.每次丢弃都要记录（第 2 单元），数字真实才看得到浪费的真实成本。

Figures are from an Australian report and are for teaching only. 数字来自澳大利亚报告，仅供教学。
